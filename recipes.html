Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min | Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g pancetta
  • 4 eggs
  • 100g Parmesan cheese
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package directions
  2. Fry pancetta until crispy
  3. Beat eggs with grated Parmesan
  4. Toss hot pasta with pancetta and egg mixture
  5. Season with black pepper and serve immediately

Homemade Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep: 15 min | Cook: 12 min | Servings: 24 cookies

Ingredients:

  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. Cream butter and sugars together
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla
  4. Mix in flour, baking soda, and salt
  5. Stir in chocolate chips
  6. Drop by rounded tablespoons onto baking sheets
  7. Bake 9-11 minutes until golden brown

Vegetable Stir Fry

Prep: 15 min | Cook: 10 min | Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated

Instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a large wok or skillet
  2. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry 30 seconds
  3. Add vegetables, stir-fry 5-7 minutes
  4. Add soy sauce and toss to coat
  5. Serve hot over rice or noodles
